# Brand notes for paid Meta ads

Audiences, links, terms and cautions per brand. No IDs, budgets or results here: find IDs with the connector and keep money in the private budget log.

## Explorazone (Coreaxis, Bowthorpe, Norwich)

- Spelling: "Explorazone", one word, capital E. During the 2026 rebrand, end cards may still say "Formerly Exploring Science".
- Audiences, one ad set each:
  - **Families**: adults 25 to 65 under Advantage+ audience (age 25 hard floor, 25 to 65 as suggestions). Takes every creative aimed at parents or the whole family.
  - **Grandparents**: adults 50 to 65+, Advantage+ audience off so the age floor holds. Takes only the grandparent and three-generation creatives. Grandparent creatives never go in an ad set aimed at parents.
- Location: a radius around the venue (about 60 km covers Norwich and most of Norfolk), people living there or recently there. Copy the place key from the working ad set.
- Exclude existing customers and existing leads when the offer is "new customers only".
- Landing page: the official offer page on explorazone.co.uk (for buy 2, get the 3rd free: `/buy-2-get-3-free/`), with UTMs `utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=<campaign>&utm_content=<ID>`. Test any other link first; the n8n practice sign-up webhook returned 404 in October 2026.
- Button: Sign up, because the offer needs a sign-up.
- Required terms in every primary text for buy 2, get the 3rd free: the ticket price, "New customers only", "Sign up to claim" (or "Sign up first at explorazone.co.uk"), "Max 9 per group", "T&Cs apply", and the dates. Add the early-end wording when the director has approved it.
- Instagram: @explorazonelab, connected to the Page (confirmed in an ad preview on 21 Oct 2026). Meta attaches it to every creative made with the Page.
- Seasonal windows: launch each drop only inside its own dates (half term, Christmas, January, February). Later drops wait.

## STEMEX (STEM Centre Ltd)

- Paid ads speak to parents and carers, schools and commissioners; never target or address children.
- Photos of children only with documented marketing consent, in line with the safeguarding policy; when unsure, use hands, backs of heads or no children.
- Short Breaks and Alternative Provision are council-funded services: no prices in ads unless the director approves the wording, and nothing that implies eligibility or outcomes.

## Muffin Break (Mahar Coffee Ltd, a Foodco franchise)

- The franchisor's brand rules and approvals come first; check whether Foodco must approve paid ads before anything goes live.
- The UK rules on advertising less healthy food and drink (from 5 January 2026) can restrict paid online ads showing products such as muffins, cakes and sugary drinks. Check with Foodco before product-led ads.
- Local radius around the café; no health claims.

## Cafe UFO (UFO Food Ltd, including Wraps and Wings)

- The same less healthy food advertising check applies to product-led ads (fried food, sugary drinks).
- Delivery and dark-kitchen offers need the delivery platform's terms in the ad if they are part of the offer.
- Local radius; allergen claims only with evidence.
