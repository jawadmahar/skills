# Pre-posting checklist (UK)

Go through every line before Gate 2 approval. Report anything that fails or is unknown. Do not mark something as passed on assumption.

## People and safeguarding

- [ ] **No identifiable child** appears unless written parental or carer consent covering social media use is on file for that child. Images of identifiable people are personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This is especially important for STEMEX, Short Breaks, Alternative Provision and Explorazone footage.
- [ ] No children's names, school uniforms or badges, or anything that shows where a named child will be.
- [ ] Location tags on posts showing children are not added in real time. Post after the session has ended.
- [ ] Staff or customers who appear have agreed to it.
- [ ] Stock footage people are not presented as your own customers, pupils or staff.

## Claims, prices and reviews

- [ ] Every factual claim is true and you hold evidence for it (CAP Code rules 3.1 and 3.7). The CAP Code covers businesses' own social media posts, not only paid adverts.
- [ ] Prices shown to consumers include VAT and any compulsory extras, with key conditions stated (CAP Code 3.18).
- [ ] No fake, invented or incentivised-but-undisclosed reviews or testimonials. These are banned under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024.
- [ ] Offers have clear dates and limits. "Limited spaces" is only used if true.
- [ ] Food posts: no allergen statements that could mislead, and no health or nutrition claims unless authorised under the retained Nutrition and Health Claims Regulation (EC) 1924/2006.
- [ ] **Before paying to boost** a post showing cakes, muffins, sweet bakery or other less-healthy food or drink: check the UK less-healthy food advertising restrictions (paid online adverts, in force since January 2026). Organic posts on your own account are not caught. For Muffin Break, check with Foodco how the franchise is treated.

## Copyright and brand

- [ ] Music is licensed for commercial use (and recorded in `music.licence`), or added from Instagram's own library at upload. Using other people's music without a licence infringes the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and can get the post muted or the account restricted.
- [ ] Footage is your own, or from Pexels with its record in `credits.json`. No clips taken from other social accounts, TV or films.
- [ ] Brand names are correct: "Explorazone" (one word, capital E), STEMEX, Muffin Break, Cafe UFO.
- [ ] Muffin Break content follows Foodco franchise brand guidelines and has any approval the franchise agreement requires.
- [ ] No other companies' logos or trade marks are prominent without permission.

## Disclosure

- [ ] **AI label is on** if the voice is synthetic (Azure or Edge) or any visuals are AI-generated. Meta requires disclosure of realistic AI-generated audio or video. Use the AI label toggle when posting by hand, or `--ai-label` with the API.
- [ ] Any paid partnership, gifted product or influencer collaboration is labelled as an advert ("Ad" or the paid partnership tool), as required by the CAP Code and CMA guidance.

## Quality and accessibility

- [ ] Watched the whole video with sound on, on a phone.
- [ ] Captions are readable, correctly spelled, in British English, with no em dashes, and not covered by the app's buttons.
- [ ] Caption text (`caption.txt`) contains no personal data, the call to action and link are correct, and there are no more than 30 hashtags.
- [ ] The opening two seconds work without sound.
