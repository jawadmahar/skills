## What the brand is
An indoor, hands-on science attraction in Bowthorpe, Norwich, with immersive rooms (laser room, sky maze, garden of light, heat vision, AR sandbox, robot waiters and more) and a café. Formerly Exploring Science; renamed Explorazone during April to June 2026. Spelling is always "Explorazone": capital E, one word.

## Audiences
- Families with primary-age children (the core)
- Grandparents taking grandchildren out (strong performers in 2026 data)
- Three generations together
- Rainy and cold-weather day planners (an indoor attraction wins when the weather is bad)

## Voice
- Warm, playful, local, a little cheeky; written like a parent talking to another parent.
- Short lines, real situations (rain on the window, bored children, the group chat), one idea per video.
- Say: "indoors", "all weather", "hands-on", the attraction names, "Bowthorpe, Norwich".
- Avoid: jargon, "edutainment", exclamation overload, claims we cannot back up.
- Claims to keep true: "fun for six hours" and similar visit-length lines (confirm typical visit length before reusing), "all indoors".
