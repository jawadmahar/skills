# STEMEX profile

Status: Provisional, not confirmed by the brand owner. Find the brand guidelines before the first STEMEX job.
Last checked: 18 October 2026 (nothing gathered yet)

## Where to look first
Drive: "STEMEX brand", "STEMEX logo", "STEM Centre brand guidelines", "STEMEX style guide", "Short Breaks flyer", "Alternative Provision brochure". Website and existing STEMEX ads for colours, fonts and photos in use.

## What the brand is
STEM Centre Ltd's children's education brand: STEM workshops and clubs, Short Breaks, and Alternative Provision.

## Audiences (to confirm)
- Parents and carers of school-age children
- Families of children with SEND, often via Short Breaks
- Schools and local authority commissioners for Alternative Provision

## Voice (provisional)
- Encouraging, calm and reassuring; inclusive; practical about what children actually do and make.
- Avoid hype and deficit language about SEND; describe support plainly and positively.
- Never overstate funding, eligibility or outcomes.

## Safeguarding and consent
- Only photos with documented marketing consent; prefer hands, projects and backs of heads.
- Ads aimed at adults only.

## Concepts likely to fit
- Notebook series: a child's workshop journal ("Today I built...")
- Rain series: a rainy holiday-club day that turns into a build day
- Before and after reveals of real projects (with permission)

## Open questions
- Brand book, logo files and colours
- Current programmes, dates and prices to advertise
- Any local authority branding or approval rules for Short Breaks and Alternative Provision
