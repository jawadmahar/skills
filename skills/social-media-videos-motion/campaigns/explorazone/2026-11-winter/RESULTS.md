# Results: Explorazone winter 2026-27

Money figures, account ids and cost per lead are kept out of this file on purpose (public repository). The private scorecard stays with the director.

## First read: 1 to 9 November 2026 (period 1, November)

**What ran.** 38 videos (W01 to W18 and W51 to W70, less two paused early) and 20 stills (S51 to S70) went live on 1 November in four campaigns: Families videos, Families stills, Grandparents videos, Grandparents stills. All four use the awareness (reach) objective with a small daily budget each. The long-running lead campaign kept running beside them with its older ads. The half-term videos also ran for a week from 25 October in a reach campaign.

**How to read it.** Reach campaigns buy the cheapest impressions, so they can rank creatives on attention but say nothing about sign-ups or sales. Each ad had only a few thousand impressions, so treat this as a first signal, not a verdict. The Meta connector has no 3-second plays field; "hook" below is 25% plays divided by impressions, which favours shorter videos, so compare within one length.

**Findings.**
1. The objective mattered more than any creative. In the same nine days the lead campaign produced all of the sign-ups; the four reach campaigns produced almost no site visits for roughly twice the money.
2. Most viewers leave in the first seconds. About one viewer in ten reached the quarter mark and about one in a hundred reached the end, which is where the offer card sits. The stills, which show the offer all the time, drew about three times the link-click rate of the videos in the same set-up.
3. Shorter won. 8-second videos held roughly twice the share of viewers that 15-second videos did, at the quarter mark and at the end.
4. Formats that read at a glance led: weather forecast, lock-screen notifications, stopwatch, storm warning, POV captions, then and now. Slow builds trailed: quiz cards, search typing, map, letters and notes, fog.
5. The director's favourites ("In my day" film, "Operation" dossier) sat mid-table for attention in the grandparents campaign. The half-term favourites (rain window, notebook letters) were also in the lower half; POV and forecast led there too.
6. Among stills, the letter to Grandad took most of the delivery and most of the clicks in the grandparents set; forecast, boarding pass and ring-road map led the families set. Click counts per still are small.

**Decisions proposed to the director (9 Nov).**
- Put the six to eight best creatives into the lead campaign as a new ad set and judge them on sign-ups.
- Show the offer in the first two seconds of every new video (a badge or the opening line), not only on the end card.
- Make new videos 8 to 10 seconds unless the story needs longer; cut 8-second versions of the favourites.
- Keep pairing each video with its still.

Ad names in Meta did not follow the copy sheet (`EXZ_W56_...` rather than `EXZ_WIN27_W56_...`), so results were matched on the W and S numbers.
