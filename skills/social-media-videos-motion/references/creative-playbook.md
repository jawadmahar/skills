## 1. Andromeda and why diversity wins

Meta's ad delivery (the Andromeda retrieval system, rolled out through 2025) decides which ads to consider for each person largely from the creative itself. In practice:

- Near-duplicates (same footage and hook with a different headline or colour) get grouped and compete as one ad. Uploading ten of them behaves like uploading one.
- Genuinely different creatives (different people, situations, formats, looks, music and lengths) let Meta find different pockets of the audience, which lowers cost per result.
- So: one broad ad set, Advantage+ placements, 8 to 20 distinct videos, and a refresh with new concepts every 2 to 4 weeks rather than small edits.

## 2. The diversity matrix

Give each video one value per dimension. No two videos should share more than two values, and never the same audience, hook type and look together.

| Dimension | Example values |
|---|---|
| Audience | Parents 25 to 44, grandparents 55+, three generations, teens, couples, home educators and schools, SEN families, office teams |
| Hook type | Relatable moment, question, POV, numbered list ("activity #1"), outside vs inside contrast, surprise reveal, price maths, countdown, handwritten note, chat or notification, weather forecast, challenge, myth-buster, quote |
| Look | Rain on glass, notebook and handwriting, phone UI, 3D cards or portals, calendar or planner, big-number typography, photo-led POV with captions, retro or nostalgic, fridge collage, ticket or coupon |
| Mood and setting | Cosy indoors, bright and loud, calm and wonder, cheeky, nostalgic, festive |
| Music | Playful acoustic, upbeat pop, chill electronic, cinematic build, retro or jazz, piano lo-fi, festive |
| Length | 6 to 8 s, 10 to 12 s, 13 to 16 s, 18 to 25 s |

Write the plan as a table (ID, series, concept, audience, hook, look, photos, music mood, length) and check it against this before building.

## 3. Anatomy of a 10 to 15 second video

| Time | Job | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 1.5 s | Hook | Movement plus one big short line. No logo-first intros. The first frame must work as a still, because it is the thumbnail. |
| 1.5 to 6 s | Story or proof | One idea, told with real photos or a recognisable moment |
| 6 to 9 s | Variety | Two to four big photos of different attractions or products, each labelled |
| Last 3 to 4 s | End card | Logo, headline, offer, price chips, dates, button, footer, terms (the engine's `endCard`) |

Leave each text beat up long enough to read twice: about 0.3 seconds per word plus one second.

## 4. Layout and safe zones (1080 x 1920)

- Reels cover the top 14% (0 to 270 px) and bottom 35% (1248 to 1920 px) with app UI; Stories cover less. Keep every word, logo and button between y 270 and y 1248, and at least 60 px from the sides.
- Fill the covered areas with things that do not need reading: a scrolling ticker at the top, a drifting photo strip at the bottom, texture or a background photo. Empty bands look unfinished (director feedback, Oct 2026).
- Type sizes: headlines 86 to 120 px, supporting lines 36 to 50 px, legal text 20 px minimum.
- Contrast: light on dark or dark on light; add a soft shadow behind text on photos.

## 5. Copy

- Hooks: seven words or fewer, in the voice of a real person, not a brochure.
- Offer: exactly as approved. Show the maths as chips ("3 for £36").
- Primary text (Meta field): the hook sentence, one or two sentences of detail, then offer, dates and key terms. Keep the first line under about 125 characters so it survives the "more" cut.
- Headline (Meta field): 40 characters or fewer.
- Button: match the objective (Sign up, Book now, Order now, Learn more).
- Anything named in the copy must be visible in the video. When you swap a photo, re-read the copy.
- British English, no em or en dashes, prices include VAT.

## 6. Photos

- Big and colourful; hero shots at least 1,000 px wide on screen.
- Vary photos across the set so no single image dominates; track which photo each video leads with.
- Label attractions or products on cards (for example LASER ROOM) so a silent viewer knows what they are looking at.
- Real places, products and people beat stock.

## 7. Sound, accessibility and lengths

- Design for sound off (all meaning in on-screen text), but every video still gets music, because Reels are mostly watched with sound on.
- No flashing faster than three times a second; keep strong contrast.
- Mix lengths across the set (see the matrix). For Stories, under 15 seconds avoids a split.

## 8. Launch notes for the person posting

Put these in the pack (the pack generator includes them by default):

1. Every video in one ad set: broad targeting, Advantage+ placements, Reels and Stories included.
2. One ad per video, ad name exactly as in the ad copy sheet (`PREFIX_ID_slug`), so results trace back to the creative.
3. Leave them 5 to 7 days before judging. Pause the clear losers (weak hook rate and no results), keep the winners running.
4. Tell the skill what happened ("smvm feedback") once the ads have about a week of data.
