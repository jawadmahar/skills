# 01 - Context and History (9 to 22 November 2026)

## The offer
A free one-hour STEM taster session (robotics, coding, science) for ages 4 to 16, in groups of up to 6, on Saturdays and Sundays at Castle Quarter, Norwich. Families who attend are offered a paid block of sessions, sent as a £70 payment link.

## Benchmarks from last year

| Free Workshop (Nov 2025 to Jun 2026) | £4,151 | 1,528 | £2.72 |

The realistic target is £2 to £3 per lead. All of last year's winning ads led with the word **FREE** in the headline.

## What went wrong with the November taster campaign (old campaign 120252110235640363)
- **Setup:** one ad set, 15 ads, £15 a day, optimised for "conversion leads" (Meta's quality-lead setting), all Facebook and Instagram placements.
- **Results, 9 to 21 November:** £183.79 spent, 21 form leads, **£8.75 per lead**.
  - 9 to 13 Nov: £4.32 per lead
  - 14 to 17 Nov: £15.45 per lead
  - 18 to 21 Nov: £20.23 per lead, while the cost per 1,000 views rose from £6.77 to £11.08

**Causes found:**
1. **The best ads were paused by staff.** "03 Welcome Back" (£4.20 per lead) was paused on 13 Nov, and "05 Saturdays", which produced 10 of the 21 leads, was paused on 19 Nov.
2. **Frequent edits** on 10, 11, 13, 19 and 21 Nov kept resetting Meta's learning.
3. **The ad wording was wrong.**
   - None of the 10 headlines or 10 texts said "free".
   - The main text on every ad was aimed at grandparents, although 89% of the audience was mums.
   - Half the text pool was off-brand template copy ("your students", "curriculum-aligned toys").
   - The ads said ages 5 to 17.
4. **"Conversion leads" had no quality data to learn from.** GHL only sends a basic "Lead" event to Meta and no later stages.
5. **The Norwich audience is saturated.** Last year's campaign showed its ads to each person an average of 9.2 times.

**Who the old campaign actually reached:**
- 89% of spend went on women, and all 21 leads were women.
- Women aged 35 to 44 took 47% of spend, at £7.84 per lead.
- People aged 55 and over had the highest click rate (about 4.5%), which points to grandparents.
- The Facebook feed took 77% of spend and produced 19 of the 21 leads. Instagram cost £29 for 1 lead.

**On £184 of spend, that works out at:**
- about £14 per taster booked
- about £23 per taster attended
- £92 per block sold so far

**The biggest leak is follow-up, not the ads:** 46% of leads sit in "Chasing". The fix is an automatic SMS with a booking link (the brief has gone to Eman) and chasing the 11 leads this week.

## Decisions Jawad made on 22 Nov
- Widen the radius to 50 km.
- Stop Instagram and run on the Facebook feed only.
- Target parents of children aged roughly 3 to 14, using Meta's bands 3-5, 6-8, 9-12 and 13-17 as hints only.
- Switch from "conversion leads" to standard leads.
- Reuse last year's data through a lookalike of families who attended or bought.
- Make "free", ages 4 to 16 and Saturdays and Sundays explicit in every ad.
- Freeze edits for 14 days and review on 6 Dec.

The plan was reviewed twice by an independent Claude reviewer. Both times the verdict was "approved with fixes", and the fixes are folded into `03_OPEN_ACTIONS.md`.
