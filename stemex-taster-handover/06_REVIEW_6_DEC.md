## Decision guide
- **Targets met:** keep running. Consider a budget rise of no more than 20% at a time, and plan fresh creatives for week 3.
- **Cost per lead good but bookings poor:** the problem is follow-up, not the ads. Check the SMS automation and speed of response.
- **Cost per lead over £8 after 14 days:** refresh the creatives, starting with last year's illustrated "Free STEM Workshop" style images. Consider the higher-intent form.
- Turn off the weakest ads only if there is clear evidence, at least £20 spent per ad. Make the changes in one go, not spread over several days.
