# Youth Jobs Grant - Research Prompts

Prepared 18 November 2026. Both prompts share one output template so the Gemini and Claude results can be compared line by line in the Fable 5.2 review.

---

## PROMPT 1 - GEMINI (Deep Research)

```
You are a UK grant compliance researcher. Use Deep Research. Today is 18 November 2026.

SUBJECT
The DWP "Youth Jobs Grant" (part of the Youth Guarantee), Great Britain. Opened 30 June 2026, closes 31 October 2028. Up to £3,000 per job outcome, paid in stages. Listed on GOV.UK Find a Grant (note: the advert may have moved from /grants/youth-jobs-grant-1 to /grants/youth-jobs-grant-2, confirm which is live).

CONTEXT
A Norwich-based owner-managed group intends to apply separately for each of these limited companies, in this order:
1. Mahar Coffee Ltd (Muffin Break franchise cafe)
2. Coreaxis2 Ltd (Explorazone, science amusement centre, Bowthorpe)
3. UFO Food Ltd (Cafe UFO and dark kitchen)
4. STEMEX Ltd and/or STEM Centre Ltd (children's STEM education, Short Breaks, Alternative Provision)
5. Mahaar Bros Ltd (Marmalade)
All share a common director/owner. Some are small (under 10 staff).

PRIMARY SOURCES TO PRIORITISE (in this order)
- GOV.UK: Youth Jobs Grant grant guidance, Terms and Conditions (v1.0 and any later version), Employer FAQs, draft Grant Funding Agreement
- GOV.UK Find a Grant advert (all tabs: Summary, Eligibility, Objectives, Dates, How to apply, Supporting information)
- business.gov.uk "Build Your Future Workforce" / DWP employer toolkit
- Hansard written statements (e.g. HCWS163, 30 June 2026), PQ answers, House of Commons Library briefing CBP-10827
- Subsidy Control Act 2022 and statutory guidance
Use secondary sources (IFS, Bright Blue, Youth Employment UK, trade bodies, payroll firms) only to fill gaps, and label them as secondary.

QUESTIONS TO ANSWER

A. Employer eligibility
1. Exact employer criteria: GB location, minimum trading period, HMRC PAYE registration, DWP checks, any sector or organisation-type exclusions.
2. Is there any rule on newly incorporated companies or companies with under 6 months of trading or payroll history?
3. Minimum headcount to apply. What applies to an employer with fewer than 4 employees?
4. Headcount caps on funded hires (e.g. max 2 for 4 to 9 employees, 20% of headcount for 10+). How is headcount measured (PAYE count, FTE, date of measurement)? Is it "at any one time" or total over the scheme?

B. Group and connected companies (critical)
5. How does DWP treat companies with a common owner/director? Do "associated organisations" or "single economic operator" rules combine headcount, caps or funding across the group?
6. Can each limited company apply under its own Find a Grant / One Login account? Can one person administer several applications?
7. The £25m cap across Youth Jobs Grant and Jobs Guarantee over three financial years: how is it calculated for a group, and what declaration is required?
8. Subsidy control: what is the Minimal Financial Assistance (MFA) position, what other public funding must be declared, and over what period?
9. Can a franchisee (Mahar Coffee Ltd, Muffin Break / Foodco franchise) apply in its own right? Any franchisor consent issues?

C. Young person eligibility and recruitment route
10. Exact criteria: age 18 to 24 (at what date), Universal Credit, length of time out of work or on UC, right to work.
11. Recruitment route: must candidates come only via Jobcentre Plus referral? Can an employer advertise publicly and then ask the Jobcentre to confirm eligibility? Are retrospective claims ever allowed?
12. Exclusions: existing or former employees, relatives, people previously on Kickstart, people on another funded scheme, agency workers.
13. How does an employer ask a Jobcentre to send candidates (Norwich / Norfolk Jobcentre Plus, DWP employer adviser contact route)?

D. Job requirements
14. Minimum hours (25 per week?), minimum duration (4 months?), pay (National Minimum Wage / National Living Wage rate bands for 18 to 20 and 21+ from April 2026).
15. Contract type: permanent vs fixed term, zero-hours, seasonal, term-time only, split across two companies in the same group.
16. "Additionality": what counts as a new job vs replacing existing staff or displacing hours? Can a vacancy created by a leaver qualify?
17. Can the role be an apprenticeship? Can it be combined with the apprenticeship incentive, Jobs Guarantee, Access to Work, Disability Confident, or employer NI relief for under-21s / apprentices under 25?
18. Any DBS or safeguarding implications for roles working with children (relevant to STEM Centre / STEMEX)?

E. Payments, evidence and clawback
19. Exact payment schedule: £1,800 at week 6 and £1,200 at week 18, or month 2 and month 5? Confirm from primary source and note any conflict.
20. How is employment verified (HMRC RTI)? What does the employer submit at each stage?
21. What happens if the young person leaves, is dismissed, or hours drop below the threshold before each payment point?
22. Clawback, repayment, suspension and audit terms. Record-keeping period.
23. Tax treatment of the grant (taxable income for Corporation Tax? VAT outside scope?).

F. Application process
24. Step by step: One Login, Find a Grant application, questions asked, documents needed, timescales to decision, when the vacancy must be posted relative to approval.
25. Is approval per employer or per vacancy? Is there a funding pot that can close early ("subject to funding remaining available")?

G. Statistics and status
26. Latest published figures: employers applied/approved, young people started, grants paid, regional (East of England / Norfolk) breakdown, total budget, target (60,000 over three years?).
27. Any changes to the scheme since launch (guidance version history, T&C updates, eligibility tweaks, Budget 2026 announcements).
28. Known practical problems reported by employers (delays, referral quality, payment issues).

OUTPUT FORMAT (use exactly this structure)
1. Executive summary (max 10 lines)
2. Rules table: Rule | Exact wording or figure | Source URL | Date/version of source | Primary or secondary | Confidence (High/Medium/Low)
3. Answers to questions A1 to G28, numbered, each with source link
4. Conflicts between sources: list each conflict, which source you trust and why
5. Group strategy implications for the five companies above (who is likely eligible, likely cap per company, risks)
6. Unknowns: questions the published material does not answer, phrased so they can be put to DWP
7. Full source list with access dates

RULES
- British English. No em dashes.
- Never guess. If not found, write "NOT FOUND" and list where you looked.
- Quote exact wording for any eligibility rule, cap, payment trigger or clawback clause.
- Distinguish the Youth Jobs Grant from the Jobs Guarantee and the Apprenticeship incentive throughout.
```

## PROMPT 2 - CLAUDE (Research mode, web search on)

```
Today is 18 November 2026. Act as a UK public-funding compliance analyst. Turn on web search and research thoroughly before answering. Read the primary GOV.UK documents in full, not summaries of them.

TASK
Produce a verified rulebook and group application strategy for the DWP Youth Jobs Grant (Youth Guarantee, Great Britain; opened 30 June 2026, closes 31 October 2028; up to £3,000 per job outcome, paid in stages).

APPLICANT GROUP (common owner/director, Norwich, Norfolk)
Applications planned per limited company, in this order:
1. Mahar Coffee Ltd - Muffin Break franchise cafe (Foodco franchise)
2. Coreaxis2 Ltd - Explorazone science amusement centre, Bowthorpe
3. UFO Food Ltd - Cafe UFO and dark kitchen
4. STEMEX Ltd or STEM Centre Ltd - children's STEM education, Short Breaks, Alternative Provision (advise which one is the better applicant, or both)
5. Mahaar Bros Ltd - Marmalade

SOURCES, IN ORDER OF AUTHORITY
1. GOV.UK Youth Jobs Grant collection: grant guidance, Terms and Conditions (all versions), Employer FAQs, draft Grant Funding Agreement
2. GOV.UK Find a Grant advert (check whether youth-jobs-grant-1 or youth-jobs-grant-2 is the live listing)
3. business.gov.uk "Build Your Future Workforce" and DWP employer toolkit
4. Hansard (HCWS163, 30 June 2026), PQ answers, Commons Library CBP-10827
5. Subsidy Control Act 2022 and statutory guidance; HMRC guidance on grant tax treatment
6. Secondary commentary only to fill gaps, clearly labelled

WHAT I NEED VERIFIED
1. Employer eligibility: trading period, PAYE, GB, sector exclusions, minimum headcount, treatment of new or very small companies.
2. Hire caps by headcount (reported as max 2 for 4 to 9 staff, 20% for 10+). How headcount is measured and whether caps are "at any one time".
3. Group rules: associated organisations, single economic operator, whether headcount or caps aggregate across commonly owned companies, the £25m three-year cap across Youth Jobs Grant and Jobs Guarantee, subsidy control / MFA declarations.
4. Franchisee eligibility (Mahar Coffee Ltd).
5. Young person criteria: 18 to 24, Universal Credit, six months out of work, right to work. Exclusions (existing/former staff, relatives, other schemes).
6. Recruitment route: Jobcentre Plus referral only? Can we advertise and have DWP confirm eligibility? Retrospective claims? How to engage Norwich/Norfolk Jobcentre employer advisers.
7. Job requirements: 25+ hours, 4+ months, NMW/NLW (April 2026 rates), contract types allowed, additionality and displacement rules, apprenticeships, stacking with other incentives and NI reliefs.
8. Payments: confirm the exact schedule (£1,800 + £1,200; at week 6 / week 18 or month 2 / month 5), RTI verification, what triggers each payment, early leaver rules, clawback, audit, record retention, tax treatment.
9. Application process: step by step on Find a Grant / One Login, information and documents required, decision timescales, whether approval is per employer or per vacancy, risk of funding closing early.
10. Statistics: latest published uptake (employers, starts, payments, budget, regional data for East of England/Norfolk), target of 60,000 over three years, any scheme changes since launch.
11. Safeguarding: DBS and Keeping Children Safe in Education implications for under-25 hires in the STEM education companies.

OUTPUT (use exactly this structure so it can be compared with another model's report)
1. Executive summary (max 10 lines)
2. Rules table: Rule | Exact wording or figure | Source URL | Date/version | Primary or secondary | Confidence
3. Numbered answers to points 1 to 11, with a citation on every factual claim
4. Conflicts between sources and your resolution
5. Per-company assessment table: Company | Likely eligible (Y/N/Uncertain) | Why | Likely max funded hires | Maximum grant value | Key risk
6. Questions to put to DWP before applying
7. Source list with access dates

RULES
- British English, no em dashes.
- If you cannot verify something from a primary source, say "UNVERIFIED" and do not fill the gap with assumptions.
- Quote verbatim for caps, eligibility tests, payment triggers and clawback clauses.
- Keep the Youth Jobs Grant separate from the Jobs Guarantee and apprenticeship incentives.
```
